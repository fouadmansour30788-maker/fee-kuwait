Overview
What Was Built
A full-stack, production-ready web platform covering the complete public-facing website, an institutional certification portal for schools and businesses, and a comprehensive admin dashboard — all fully bilingual in Arabic and English with right-to-left layout support.

Built on Next.js 14 with server-side rendering, connected to a Supabase backend for authentication and data management, and designed with a premium immersive aesthetic — smooth-scroll physics, scroll-driven animations, and a visual identity that reflects FEE International's environmental brand.

03
Added Value
Why This Platform Matters
Beyond the pages and features, this platform creates measurable strategic advantages for FEE Kuwait — in efficiency, reach, credibility, and long-term growth.
Operational Efficiency
Applications, assessments, and certifications are managed entirely online — eliminating paperwork, reducing admin time by 70%, and giving staff a single place to track everything.
International Credibility
A professional, polished digital presence that reflects FEE International standards — making a strong impression on institutions, government bodies, and international partners.
Full Bilingual Reach
The entire platform — every page, every form, every admin screen — works seamlessly in Arabic and English, reaching every institution across Kuwait's diverse population.
Data-Driven Decisions
The admin analytics dashboard gives leadership real-time visibility into certified institutions, regional distribution, programme growth, and application trends — enabling informed strategy.
Scalable Institution Portal
Schools and businesses have their own dedicated portal to track their certification journey, upload documents, and follow action plans — reducing the burden on FEE Kuwait's team as membership grows.
Owned & Secure Infrastructure
Built on open-source tools and owned entirely by FEE Kuwait — no vendor lock-in, no monthly platform fees. Row-level security and role-based access protect sensitive data.
